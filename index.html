Drooling Station, Vikas Nagar

Located strategically at Commander Chowk on Ranhola Road, Drooling Station is a local culinary hotspot dedicated to quick, pocket-friendly, and mouth-watering street food.

From thin-skinned Himalayan steamed momos with fiery garlic sauce to sizzling woks tossing spicy chowmein and crunchy kathi rolls, we prioritize robust Delhi flavors, hot counter service, and fresh daily preparation.
